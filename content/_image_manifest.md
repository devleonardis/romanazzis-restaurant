## Notes for bulk download
- Filenames above use the highest-resolution / unsuffixed variant found in each `srcset` where available (e.g. `foo.webp` at full width rather than `foo-300x200.webp`); a couple of entries only ever appeared at a `-768x512` or `-scaled` size in the crawled HTML — those are noted as-is and are still the largest available copy found.
- No genuine PDF files exist anywhere on the site. The digital menu and wine list live entirely inside the third-party Pienissimo widget (see `_meta.md`), and the "Menù di Natale" is a raster image, not a document.
- All URLs share the same origin (`https://www.romanazzis.it/wp-content/uploads/...`), so a simple `wget -i` / `curl` loop over this list will work without auth.
